2209-A Research Project Support Programme for Undergraduate Students and 2209-B Industry Oriented Research Project Support Programme for Undergraduate Students 2026 Calls are Open for Application
+
-
0
09 Oct 2026
Applications for the 2026 calls will remain open until 11 November 2026.
The Scientific and Technological Research Council of Türkiye (TÜBİTAK) continues to support efforts to strengthen Türkiye’s position as an independent and leading country in science, technology, and innovation, and to enhance its global competitiveness, with significant contributions from the country’s scientists.
The 2209-A Research Project Support Programme for Undergraduate Students and the 2209-B Industry-Oriented Research Project Support Programme for Undergraduate Students continue to provide undergraduate and associate degree students with research and project management experience. Applications for the 2026 calls of both programmes are now open.
The 2026 call calendar for the 2209 programmes is as follows:
